## Functional Requirements  
**Voucher Photo Upload:** The system shall allow authorized users to upload voucher photos.  
**Photo Association:** The system shall link each photo to the correct voucher or transaction.  
**View Photo:** Users shall be able to view uploaded voucher photos.  
**Download Photo:** Authorized users shall be able to download voucher photos.  
**Update Photo:** Authorized users shall be able to replace an existing voucher photo.  
**Delete Photo:** Authorized users shall be able to delete voucher photos.  
**Multiple Photos:** The system shall allow multiple photos for a single voucher when required.  
**Upload History:** The system shall record who uploaded or modified each voucher photo.  
**Link Transaction:** The system shall allow user to link a transaction to a voucher
## Non-Functional Requirements  
**Security:** The system shall prevent unauthorized access to voucher photos.  
**Performance:** The system shall upload and display voucher photos quickly.  
**Reliability:** The system shall store voucher photos safely without data loss.  
**Data Integrity:** The system shall ensure each photo remains linked to the correct voucher.  
**Availability:** The voucher photo service shall be available whenever the system is operational.  
**Scalability:** The system shall handle a growing number of voucher photos efficiently.  
**Privacy:** The system shall protect sensitive information contained in voucher photos.  
**Backup & Recovery:** The system shall regularly back up voucher photos and support data recovery.  
**Usability:** The upload and management process shall be simple and user-friendly.
